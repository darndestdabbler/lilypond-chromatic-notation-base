Session 33: Key-signature ledgers from the notation's ledger rules

Why. The Configurator's session 49 engine round trip (cnb/tests/cnb-lilypond-tests.js, "mark table (engine)") found that a key signature's noteheads take their ledger lines from the engine's own rule, cnb:notehead-ledgers (cnb/keysig.ly): cnb:proximity-rule on the head's staff position rounded to a whole number. Since the Configurator's LY spec 1.3.9 §5.7 every export carries the notation's ledgers by pitch, cnb:ledgers-by-semitone (engine semitone, middle C = 0, → ledger staff positions), and every note's head takes its entry through NoteHead.ledger-positions. The signature does not, so it disagrees with the notes and with the Configurator's canvas: on the default 4-8 staff the engraved C-major signature gives B a ledger above the staff and C and D none, where the notes, and the canvas's signature, give C and D their in-gap ledgers and B none. This holds for 'chord and 'marks alike, and a ledger past the marks also moves the signature's ink and so the first note. The operator chose to fix it here rather than copy the engine's rule into the canvas (Configurator session 49).

  1. Register.
  2. The fix. A key-signature head whose engine semitone has an entry in cnb:ledgers-by-semitone, when the export defines it, takes exactly that entry's ledgers (half staff spaces from the middle line, scaled by the magnification as today), none for an empty entry. A head with no entry, or a score with no table, keeps today's rule. This covers 'chord's heads (their semitones from cnb:keysig-pitches) and 'marks's (each mark's semi). Lengths, thickness, caps and layering are unchanged: 4/3 of the head's width, 0.2 staff spaces, round caps. cnb:ledgers-by-semitone keeps its format; if the engine needs to read it before the export defines it, give it a default of '() in cnb/config.ly and say in the handoff whether the export should set! rather than define it.
  3. Tests. Unit specs for a signature head with an entry, one with an empty entry, one with none (today's rule), and no table at all; integration specs for the default 4-8 staff's C major under 'chord and 'marks (C and D with their in-gap ledgers, B with none). Goldens on LilyPond 2.24 and 2.26 without a table stay byte-identical; any golden whose score defines the table and whose signature ledgers change is named in the handoff.
  4. A CHANGELOG.md section and version.json to 1.4.1: a patch, the signature following the notation's ledger rules that every export already carries. Write docs/session-sets/033-keysig-ledgers-by-semitone/configurator-handoff.md: what changed, a worked case, and session 34 as the release.
