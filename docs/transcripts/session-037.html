Session Plan

Session 37: Placed, coloured and layered key-signature marks

Why. The Configurator's session 53 designed one model for every key signature (its docs/planning/key-signature-designer-design.md §13), and its session 54 draws it on the canvas. A mark is a base symbol (a notehead, Emmentaler's sharp or flat, or an imported shape) and up to two added symbols, such as a dot with a tail. Each symbol is placed by a reference point plus an offset, so a one-sided symbol needs no hidden geometry to keep its dot on its stack. Signs can take a colour. Clairnote's signatures need all of it: a dot solid on a line and hollow in a space, a tail up for flats and down for sharps. The mark table's format (engine 1.4.0, sessions 30 and 33; the scale of engine 1.5.0, session 35) keeps its shape and gains fields (the design's §13.10). The Configurator's session 55 writes them.

  1. Register.
  2. Placed marks. head, symbol, sharp and flat marks take an optional last element, (at dx . dy), in staff spaces. With it, the stencil's origin goes to the column plus dx and the semitone's height plus dy, and its box is not centred on the column. A mark without it is placed exactly as today, so every table exported today engraves as before. A head's ledgers move with it.
  3. Coloured signs. A sharp or flat mark may carry a colour symbol after its scale, registered as a head's colour is: (13 1 sharp 0.6473 red-cc0000 (at 0.1 . 0.25)). A black sign writes no colour, and a sign with a colour or at writes its scale even when it is 1, so the elements keep their positions. A sign without a colour draws black, as today.
  4. **The symbol kind.** (semi column symbol <shape> <cutout> <color> [at]) is drawn as head is, through the registered stencils, solid or hollow by its cutout (#f for solid), but takes no ledgers. It serves added symbols and any symbol that takes no ledger lines.
  5. Drawing order. The bars, then every ledger, then the marks in the table's order, so the marks layered at one semitone and column (a dot and its tail) draw in order and no ledger covers a mark. A mark's ledgers come from its head only, so a symbol beside it adds none.
  6. Tests. Unit specs for each item: a mark with and without at, in x and y; a coloured sharp and flat, a black one, and a sign with at at scale 1; a solid and a hollow symbol with no ledgers; a dot and tail layered at one semitone; the drawing order. Integration specs on the default 4-8 staff in D and B♭ major with placed, coloured and layered marks. Goldens and integration specs on LilyPond 2.24 and 2.26; the key-change, transposition and system-start tests stay, and every golden without the new fields stays byte-identical.
  7. A CHANGELOG.md section and version.json to 1.6.0: a minor, since the mark table gains fields and no existing output changes. Write docs/session-sets/037-placed-keysig-marks/configurator-handoff.md: what changed, a worked case for each item, and session 38 as the release.
