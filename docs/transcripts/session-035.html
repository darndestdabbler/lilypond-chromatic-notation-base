Session 35: Sized sharps and flats, and the magnification a config sets

Why. The Configurator's session 51 draws a traditional signature's sharps and flats through the mark table, (semi column sharp) and (semi column flat), and the engine draws each one as Emmentaler's glyph at the staff's size. A sharp is then 3 staff spaces tall. On the default 4-8 staff, at 4 semitones a staff space, that is 12 semitones, an octave, far too big beside the noteheads (operator, 2026-11-11). Configurator session 52 sizes the signs in semitones, by default the least of the noteheads' mean height, one staff space and 3 semitones, measured as the gap between the sharp's bars. It needs the engine to draw a sign at a given scale.

The same session's round trips found that cnb/score-defaults.ly reads cnb:default-magnify when the engine is included, before an exported config set!s it. A plain score therefore engraves every staff at 7/4, whatever the config says. Only a staff that magnifies itself with \magnifyStaff #cnb:default-magnify gets the config's value. This affects every glyph, not only the signs, and it decides the optical size the signs come from.

  1. Register.
  2. Sized signs. A sign mark takes an optional fourth element, a positive number: (semi column sharp 0.6473). The glyph is scaled by it about its origin, on both axes (the sharp's centre line or the flat's bowl line stays at the semitone's height), before its X extent is centred on its column, as today. Without the element, or with 1, a sign draws exactly as today. Another value (not a positive number) is a warning naming the key, and the sign draws unscaled. The format note in cnb/config.ly gains the element.
  3. The magnification a config sets. A staff in a plain score engraves at cnb:default-magnify as it stands when the score is engraved, so a config included after the engine, which set!s it, takes effect. A staff's own \magnifyStaff still wins, \magnifyStaff stays idempotent, and opting out with cnb:score-defaults #f still works. How to get there is the engine's choice, for example reading the variable when each staff starts rather than when the file is included. The handoff says whether the Configurator's round trips can drop the \magnifyStaff #cnb:default-magnify they now prefix to their music.
  4. Tests. Unit specs: a sign with no scale, with 1 and with 0.5, whose box is half as wide and tall and whose origin stays at the semitone; a flat likewise; a bad scale warns. Integration specs: the default 4-8 staff in D and B♭ major with scaled signs, and a plain score including a config that set!s cnb:default-magnify to 1 and 5/2, each engraving at that magnification. Goldens on LilyPond 2.24 and 2.26 without scaled signs stay byte-identical. A golden whose config set!s a magnification other than 7/4 may change; name each one in the handoff.
  5. A CHANGELOG.md section and version.json to 1.5.0: a minor, since the mark table gains a field. Write docs/session-sets/035-sized-signs-and-magnify/configurator-handoff.md: what changed, a worked case for each item, and session 36 as the release.
