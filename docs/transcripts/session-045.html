Session 45: The breve in shape mode, a double notehead or two tied semibreves

Why. The Configurator's Noteheads step gains three designs (Configurator session 74, 2026-11-17; its docs/planning/notehead-duration-design.md, B.i, sections 1 and 6.3). In one, B.i (*hollows*), the designer's own notehead shows duration by fill, in shape mode ((repeat . #f), one head a note): a crotchet solid with a stem, a minim hollow with a stem, a semibreve hollow without one. For the breve the designer chooses a double notehead (two copies of the hollow head, overlapping as a repeated minim's two heads do) or two tied semibreves (two hollow heads 1 staff space apart, tied below from the middle of the first to the middle of the second, as engine 1.7.0 ties a repeat-mode breve's two groups). Today a shape-mode breve engraves as one head, so neither reaches the engraving. The brief is the Configurator's docs/planning/shape-mode-breve-brief.md; this session follows it. Configurator session 76 draws and exports the setting, and its engine round trip waits on this session's release: on engine 1.9.0 the variable is unbound and LilyPond stops.

Nothing changes unless the setting is set, so every export written today engraves as before.

  1. Register.
  2. The setting. A new cnb:breve-style (cnb/config.ly), a symbol, 'single by default (one head, as today), 'double or 'tied. It is read only for a breve itself (duration log -1) whose resolved notehead has (repeat . #f). In repeat mode a breve stays two tied groups of four whatever the setting, and a shape-mode longa or maxima stays one head. Any other value draws as 'single with a warning naming cnb:breve-style and the value. (The brief asks for an error, "as the engine's other style settings do"; the engine's settings warn and fall back, and none stops LilyPond, so by the operator's decision of 2026-11-18 this follows the engine.) A longa or maxima engraved in shape mode, under any style, gives one warning a run, not one a note: it draws as the breve's one head, so it reads shorter than it is (backlog section 7, docs/planning/configurator-coordination-backlog.md). The warning changes no output.
  3. **'double.** In cnb/stencils.ly, two copies of the notehead's stencil, its cutout and colour included, the second starting (1 - overlap) x w after the first, w one head's X width and the overlap cnb:multinote-overlap; when that is #f, the copies are w + cnb:multinote-padding apart, as repeat mode spaces them. No tie.
  4. **'tied.** Two copies of the stencil, the second's left edge 1 staff space after the first's right edge, and one (make-tie-stencil (cons xa y0) (cons xb y0) 0.12 DOWN), xa and xb the two heads' centres and y0 the bottom of the head's Y extent, added after the head is coloured, so it stays black, as the repeat-mode breve's tie is.
  5. One note. The breve stays one NoteHead grob, and only its stencil changes, as in repeat mode: dots, spacing, ledgers (each head's cnb:ledgers-by-semitone entry, 1.5 times the whole stencil's width, as LilyPond sizes them) and the per-pitch fields behave as for any notehead. A shape's reference point (the -ref registrations) moves both heads, and the tie runs below the moved heads. The stencil cache keeps the three styles apart from each other and from repeat mode. Stems are untouched (a breve has none).
  6. Specs. Unit specs (test-unit.ly): 'single is the base stencil; 'double and 'tied have the widths, head positions and tie ends the rules give, at overlaps 1/3, 0.25 and #f; repeat mode, a shape-mode semibreve and a shape-mode longa are as today under each style; an unknown symbol draws as 'single; a shape-mode longa warns once, and a repeat-mode one does not.
