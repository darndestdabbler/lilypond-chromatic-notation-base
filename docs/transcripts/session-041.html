Session 41: The dozenal clef sized about the middle line

Why. In the Configurator's session-57 try-out the operator found the clefs too big, as the sharps and flats were, and asked that a clef's height be adjustable without the clef leaving its pitch. In its session 59 (2026-11-13) the operator picked, from mock-ups, an automatic size that follows the staff as the signs' does (LilyPond's size with each staff space drawn as the least of the noteheads' mean height, one staff space and 3 semitones: 68% of LilyPond's size on the default Configurator staff of 4 semitones per staff space), each clef scaled about an anchor point; the dozenal clef's is its middle, on the staff's middle line. A glyph clef needs nothing new: the Configurator registers its top and height. The dozenal clef does: cnb:clef-size-semitones already sets its height, but its vertical offsets stay fixed, so a numeral of another height leaves its place. Configurator session 59 writes cnb:clef-size-semitones for the dozenal clef; its engine round trip waits on this session.

Nothing changes unless cnb:clef-size-semitones is set with the dozenal clef, so every export written today engraves as before.
