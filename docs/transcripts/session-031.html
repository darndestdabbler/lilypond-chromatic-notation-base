Session 31: A key's pitch classes with double sharps and flats

Why. Session 30 found that cnb:get-key-pitch-classes (cnb/procedure.ly) misreads a key with double sharps or flats: \key dis \major gives (2 2 3 7 7 8 10), with repeated pitch classes, in place of (0 2 3 5 7 8 10). It changes the list in place, replacing every pitch class equal to a letter's natural one, so an alteration that lands on another letter's natural pitch class (E♯ on F, F𝄪 on G) can be overwritten by a later one, depending on the order of keyAlterations. Every key with at most seven sharps or flats happens to read correctly in the order LilyPond gives (session 30 checked C♯, C♭, F♯ and G♭ major and A♯, A♭ and G♯ minor from real \key events). A key with double sharps or flats is read wrongly. That reaches the configuration procedure's key argument (and so off-key marks), and a missing key's warning under 'marks. The operator asked for the fix before the release.

  1. Register.
  2. The fix. cnb:get-key-pitch-classes gives each of the seven letters its pitch class from its own alteration (natural when the alist has none), (natural + 2 × alteration) mod 12, in letter order C to B as today, accepting the ((octave . step) . alteration) form it accepts now. No other function changes.
  3. Tests. test-unit.ly's cnb:get-key-pitch-classes group keeps its cases and adds D♯ major and its order-reversed alist, a double-flat key (F♭ major), C♯ and C♭ major in LilyPond's order and reversed, and cnb:keysig-marks-key for D♯ major. Every existing golden stays byte-identical on LilyPond 2.24 and 2.26 (no existing spec has a double-sharp or double-flat key).
  4. A line in CHANGELOG.md's unreleased 1.4.0 section. Correct docs/session-sets/030-keysig-mark-list/configurator-handoff.md: every key's pitch classes are now read correctly (D♯ major's warning gives (0 2 3 5 7 8 10)), and session 32 is the release.
