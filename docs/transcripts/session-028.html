Session 28: The breve's tie below the noteheads

Why. In the Configurator's session-45 try-out the operator found the breve's tie wrong (2026-11-11, Configurator session 46): it should be below the noteheads, not in the gap between the two semibreve groups on the heads' centre line. Ideally one tie spans from the centre of the first notehead of the first group to the centre of the last notehead of the last group. Configurator session 46 draws the same tie in its preview, and its engine round trip waits on this session.

This changes what a breve (and a longa or maxima) engraves in repeat mode, so the goldens and harvests that contain one change.

  1. Register.
  2. The tie. In cnb:build-duration-stencil (cnb/stencils.ly), for groups above 1, one (make-tie-stencil (cons xa y0) (cons xb y0) 0.12 DOWN) replaces the ties in the gaps: xa is the centre of the first head of the first group, xb the centre of the last head of the last group, and y0 the bottom of the group stencil's Y extent, so the tie runs below the heads and curves down as LilyPond's ties under a note do, with LilyPond's default height limit (1.0) and ratio (0.33). The groups and the 1 staff space gaps between them are unchanged, so the X extent is too. The tie stays black (it is added after the head is coloured). With repeat #f, and for whole notes and shorter values, nothing changes.
  3. Specs. The integration specs on 2.24 and 2.26 assert that the breve's and the longa's width are unchanged and give their new lower Y extent. Regenerate the harvests and goldens that contain breves on both versions, and update the test-unit.ly case.
  4. A README line beside the repeat counts, a CHANGELOG.md 1.3.1 section, and version.json to 1.3.1: a patch release, one stencil corrected and nothing added. Write docs/session-sets/028-breve-tie-below/configurator-handoff.md, in the form of the earlier handoffs. It gives the measured tie for the default Configurator notehead at 4 semitones per staff space (its end points, lowest point and thickness, and the whole stencil's X and Y extents) and names session 29 as the release.
